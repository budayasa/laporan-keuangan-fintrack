# FinTrack Pro — Design System (Hallmark Almanac + Impeccable)

## Visual Direction & Material
- **Theme**: Hallmark **Almanac** (`editorial` / `Operate` mode)
- **Material**: Cool slate stock (`--color-paper: #dfe4ec`), pale printed ledger sheets (`--color-sheet: #f7f9fc`), deep blue-black ink (`--color-ink: #111827`), two rule weights (`1px` hairline and `2px` firm ink bar), crisp 2px architectural corners, and high-contrast semantic accents.
- **Anti-Slop Floor**:
  - Zero gradient text, zero colored glow shadows, zero glassmorphism blur, zero nested cards.
  - All interactive controls meet WCAG AAA contrast (`>= 7:1`).

## Typography
- **Display & UI**: `Hanken Grotesk` (500, 600, 700) — clear 1.25x+ step scale (`h1` 2.25rem, `h2` 1.75rem, `h3` 1.375rem, `h4` 1.1rem, `body` 1rem).
- **Editorial Labels & Table Headers**: `Newsreader` (400, 500, italic) — sentence case, untracked.
- **Figures & Numerals**: `IBM Plex Mono` (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) — right-aligned in ledger columns.

## Color Tokens
- `--color-paper`: `#dfe4ec` (Dark: `#0b111e`)
- `--color-sheet`: `#f7f9fc` (Dark: `#152033`)
- `--color-ink`: `#111827` (Dark: `#f1f5f9`)
- `--color-accent`: `#831843` (Chart magenta)
- `--color-income`: `#065f46` (Forest emerald, >7.5:1 with white text)
- `--color-expense`: `#9f1239` (Deep crimson, >7.5:1 with white text)
